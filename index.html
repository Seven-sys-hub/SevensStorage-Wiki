SEVEN'S STORAGE

A searchable, claim-based storage network for Afterlife Zombies. Store millions of items in one terminal, wire your farms and furnaces into it wirelessly, and replace laggy hopper chains with collectors that cost the server almost nothing.

Purpur / Paper 26.3Nexo furnitureGriefPrevention claimsJava & BedrockNear-zero lag

01 Overview

Every claim can have one Storage Controller. The controller is your storage: it holds items itself, and every Storage Module you connect to it adds more room. Right-click the controller to open the terminal, where you can search everything you own, take items out, put items in, and craft straight from your stock.

Around the storage you can build a wireless network: nodes that pull items out of chests and farms, feed furnaces, move items between chests, and collectors that pick up drops off the ground. Everything is paired with the Wireless Linker and works up to 500 blocks away inside claims.

Why it exists: hoppers check for items every tick, and a base full of them is one of the biggest causes of server lag. Seven's Storage does the same jobs from one task that runs once a second, so huge networks cost about as much as a few hoppers.

02 Quick start

  1. Claim your land with GriefPrevention. Storage only works inside claims.
  2. Buy a Storage Controller from the Black Market and place it inside your claim.
  3. Craft Storage Modules and place them touching the controller, or touching another module that's connected. Each adds 25,000 items.
  4. Right-click the controller to open the terminal. Click items in your own inventory to put them in; click items in the terminal to take them out.
  5. Grab a Wireless Linker and start wiring up your farms, furnaces and chests (see below).

04 Controller & terminal

Place the controller anywhere inside your claim; it needs build trust to place. Right-click it to open the terminal, titled Seven's Storage, showing every item you have stored with its total count.

  • Put items in: click items in your own inventory while the terminal is open.
  • Take items out: click an item in the terminal.
  • Search: use the search button. Java players type into an anvil screen; Bedrock players get a text form. Searching never costs XP.
  • Crafting: open the crafting screen from the terminal (see Crafting from storage).
Good to know
• One player uses a storage at a time; anyone else gets a message that it's in use.
• You need container trust in the claim to open someone else's storage.
• The controller can't be broken while it holds items. Empty it first.
• Staff opening a player's storage is logged.

05 Storage modules

Modules add capacity. Each one must touch the controller or another connected module; a module floating on its own does nothing. Build them in a wall, a tower, or any shape you like as long as the chain is unbroken.

Capacity = 5,000 (controller) + 25,000 × modules. How many modules a claim can use depends on the claim owner's rank. See Rank limits.

Tip: opening a module does nothing on purpose. Everything goes through the controller.

06 Crafting from storage

The terminal's crafting screen shows everything you can make right now from what's in storage, next to a real crafting table.

  • Click a recipe on the left to load its ingredients into the grid. Click it again to add another set, just like the vanilla recipe book.
  • Click the result to craft what's in the grid.
  • Shift + right-click the result to craft as many as your stock allows.

Ingredients come straight out of storage, so you never need to carry them. Only vanilla recipes and Seven's Storage's own recipes appear here.

07 Wireless Linker

The linker is how everything gets connected. You "load" it by right-clicking one thing, then right-click the thing it should connect to. The action bar (or chat, for long messages) always tells you what's loaded.

Right-click with the linker on…What it does
A Storage ControllerLoads that storage. Now click output nodes to pair them, or sneak + right-click a furnace to pair its whole group.
A chest or barrelLoads it as an ore chest. Sneak + right-click instead to load it as a fuel chest. Then click input nodes.
A furnace (sneaking)Pairs every node on that group of furnaces at once.
A sending Transfer Node or CollectorLoads it as the sender. Then click the receiving transfer node or a Storage Crate.
A Storage CrateLoads the crate, ready to be picked as a filter destination in a node's menu.
A Collector (sneaking)Cycles its pickup size.
Unpairing: sneak + right-click a node with an empty hand.
Rules: both ends must be inside claims, within 500 blocks, in the same world, and you need container trust at both ends. If you lose trust, your links are cut automatically.

08 Output nodes

Place an output node flat against a chest, barrel, or other container. Once paired to your storage it empties that container into storage, up to 256 items a second.

  1. Place the node on the container (it snaps flat to the face you click).
  2. Right-click your controller with the linker, then right-click the node.
  3. The light turns blue and items start flowing.

Right-click a node with an empty hand to open its menu: status, items moved, and its filter. Great on mob-farm chests, crop farms and anything else that fills a chest.

09 Input nodes & furnaces

Input nodes go on furnaces, blast furnaces and smokers. They keep the input slot topped up to 16 and the fuel slot to 8, pulling from an ore chest and a fuel chest you choose.

  1. Put an input node on each furnace.
  2. Right-click your ore chest with the linker (loads ORE), then sneak + right-click a furnace to pair the whole group.
  3. Sneak + right-click your fuel chest with the linker (loads FUEL), then sneak + right-click a furnace again.
Furnace groups: furnaces count as one group while there are no more than 2 blocks between them, so a sneak-click pairs your whole smelting array, but never a separate setup nearby. Pair the furnaces' output with output nodes to send smelted items straight to storage.

10 Transfer nodes

Transfer nodes move items from one chest to another, never into storage. Use them to double up ore and fuel chests, send farm output to a sell chest, or feed a Storage Crate.

  1. Put a transfer node on the sending chest and one on the receiving chest.
  2. Right-click the sending node with the linker, then the receiving node.

Transfer nodes never pair themselves; you always choose both ends. If the receiving area isn't loaded, the sender holds up to 1,728 items and delivers them when it loads again.

11 Collector

The collector is a floor pad that replaces hoppers. It picks up items that drop in an area around it and reaching 4 blocks up, and sends them to your storage. It doesn't tick: drops are grabbed the instant they appear, and anything that floats or falls in is swept up within a second.

  • Placing: inside your claim, on a solid floor. It pairs to the claim's storage automatically. Up to 32 per claim.
  • Size: 1×1 is always allowed; larger sizes depend on the claim owner's rank (see Rank limits). Change it in the menu or by sneak + right-clicking with the linker.
  • Show the area: open the menu and click Show the area to see a cyan outline of the pickup zone for 15 seconds. Only you see it.
  • Holding: it holds up to 1,728 items while sending. Breaking it drops what it's holding.
  • Sending to a crate: click the collector with the linker, then a Storage Crate. The crate becomes its filtered items destination; everything else still goes to storage.
Keep the floor solid. Items fall straight through the pad, so put it on a solid block with solid floor around it, not over a hole.

12 Filters

Output nodes, transfer nodes and collectors all have a filter. Right-click one with an empty hand to open its menu.

  • Add an item: click it in your own inventory. A copy goes in the filter; you keep your item. Up to 18 filter items.
  • Remove one: click it in the filter. Clear empties the whole filter.
  • Plain items (like bones) match every item of that type. Named or custom items match exact copies only.
  • Where filtered items go: right-click a chest or Storage Crate with the linker, then click the chest slot in the menu. Shift-click the slot to remove it.
When the filter chest is full, or none is set: collectors leave filtered items on the ground (they despawn normally after 5 minutes); output and transfer nodes leave them in the source container. Nothing is ever deleted.
Example: an iron farm collector with poppies in the filter: iron goes to storage, poppies stay on the ground and despawn. A skeleton farm with bones in the filter and a crate set: bones go to the crate, arrows and bows to storage.

13 Storage Crate

A single block that holds 5 chests' worth: 3 pages of 45 slots. Open it like a chest and use the arrows to change pages.

  • Take-out only. Players can't put anything in. Only transfer nodes, collectors and filters fill it, so it can't be used as temporary storage.
  • It receives items even while its area isn't loaded, and it never ticks.
  • It can't be broken until it's empty.
  • Perfect as a sell chest: send your mob drops here, empty it, /sell.

14 Storage Remote

A handheld terminal for Ascended and Immortal players, sold in the Black Market. Right-click anywhere inside a claim to open that claim's storage. No linking: walk into another of your claims and it opens that claim's storage instead.

  • You need container trust in the claim, like at the controller.
  • Doesn't work outside claims, in claims with no controller, in dungeons or in minigames.
  • Right-clicking a controller or node with it just uses that block normally.

15 Status lights & colors

Blue workingAmber waiting (storage full, nothing to send, receiver not loaded)Red not paired

Right-click a node with any item (not the linker) to see its status in chat. Dye: right-click any part with a dye to recolor it. Broken parts drop as a clean item that stacks with the others, and keep their color.

After a server restart, lights in areas that aren't loaded update the next time the area loads. They may show red until then even though the link is fine.

16 Crafting recipes

All parts except the Controller and Remote are crafted at a crafting table, or from your storage's crafting screen.

Black Market only: the Storage Controller and the Storage Remote (Ascended & Immortal) can't be crafted.

18 Claims & real estate

A storage belongs to its claim, not to a player. What happens when the claim changes:

EventWhat happens to the storage
You abandon the claimThe storage goes "wild": it's locked and nobody can open it.
Someone claims over a wild storageThey adopt it, items and all.
Your claim expiresIt's listed in the real estate shop. The terminal is locked while listed and unlocks for the buyer. A claim with a controller is priced $300,000 higher.
Staff delete the claimThe storage is wiped (staff keep a snapshot).
Moving house? Empty the controller, break everything down and rebuild in your new claim. Modules, nodes and the controller all drop as items.

19 FAQ & troubleshooting

"This claim already has a Storage Controller"

Only one per claim. Use /storage info or ask staff if you can't find it.

I placed a module but my capacity didn't change

It has to touch the controller or a connected module, and you may be at your rank's module limit.

My node's light is red

It isn't paired. Pair it again with the linker. After a restart, lights in unloaded areas can show red until the area loads.

Amber light on an output node

Your storage is full or locked. Add modules or clear space.

Items sit on my collector

Check the area with Show the area, make sure the floor is solid, and that the item isn't in the filter. A collector holding 1,728 items stops picking up until it can send.

I can't put items in the Storage Crate

That's by design. Only nodes and collectors fill it.

The remote says this claim has no storage

The remote opens the storage of the claim you're standing in. Walk into the claim with your controller.

Bedrock players

Everything works on Bedrock. Search uses a text form instead of the anvil.
